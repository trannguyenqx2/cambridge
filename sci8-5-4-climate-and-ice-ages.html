    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 5 Materials and their structure, section 5.4, pages 166–171; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 103–105. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about how the Earth’s climate has changed in the past; find out about ice ages, glacial periods and interglacial periods; look at some of the evidence that the Earth’s climate cycles between colder and warmer periods. Key words: boulder, cycled, glacial period, glaciers, ice ages, interglacial period, peat bog.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D ice field and peat bog. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
