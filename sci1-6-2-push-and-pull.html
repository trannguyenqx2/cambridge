    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 6 Forces, section 6.2, pages 115–120. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: know what pushing and pulling are; make measurements; record observations in a table; keep safe when doing science. Key words: push, pull, force, start, stop, slide, machine.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. In step 5 every object gets the same stretch of the band, and distances are simulated (1 brick is about 15 cm in this game; with the same push the box slides 9 bricks, the pencil case 6 and the book 4).
