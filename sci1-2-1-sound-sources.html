    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 2 Sound, section 2.1, pages 20–25; Workbook 1, pages 15–17. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find sources of sound; say which sound sources use electricity; make predictions and see if they are right; collect and record observations. Key words: sound, source, hear, ears, electricity, results.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping things in the 3D classroom itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The buttons and lists in the side panel only move the camera or show records; finding means tapping the thing itself. The short sounds are computer-made blips — no step ever needs the sound to be heard: every sound is also shown with waves and movement. In Step 5 the first thing a child points to is saved as their result, so a wrong first point is recorded honestly — exactly like the book's prediction-and-results table.
