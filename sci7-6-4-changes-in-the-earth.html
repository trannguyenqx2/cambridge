    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 6 Earth physics, section 6.4, pages 210–215; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 122–123. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: explain how fold mountains and volcanoes are formed; explain how earthquakes happen. Key words: active, dormant, earthquake, extinct, fold mountains, geological change, inactive, lava, magnitude, plate boundary, subduction, volcano.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D diorama or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
