    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 2 Materials and their structure, section 2.3, pages 35–39; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 26–29. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: use the particle theory to explain what happens when matter changes between states; use a model to illustrate the particle theory. Key words: attractive force, expand, heat energy, transferred.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in a particle-model lab — exploring the solid, liquid and gas models, sorting particle properties by state, heating a solid gently (it expands) and then strongly (it melts), heating a liquid until particles escape as a gas, condensing a gas on a cold mirror, and freezing a liquid back into a solid, before judging what the class particle model shows well and where it is limited. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
