    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 1 Cells, section 1.1 Plant cells, pages 2–6; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 1–4. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: name the structures in a plant cell and an animal cell and describe what they do; say which structures are only in plant cells; use a microscope to look at cells; change a plant cell model into an animal cell model. Key words: cell, cell membrane, cell wall, cellulose, chlorophyll, chloroplast, cytoplasm, magnify, mitochondria, nucleus, sap vacuole, stain.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping parts of the two big cells and the microscope on the stage, or sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the two tricky ideas in these sections: onion cells do not look green because they grow underground in the dark and have no chloroplasts, and the quickest way to tell a plant cell in a picture is to look for a cell wall.
