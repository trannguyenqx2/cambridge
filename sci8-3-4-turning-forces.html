    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.4, pages 85–92; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 51–54. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: recognise when a force causes something to turn; use the term moment; calculate the moment caused by a force. Key words: lever, moment, newton metres, pivot, turn.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D stage — finding things that turn, naming the lever and pivot, working out the door moment, balancing the seesaw, sorting turning from non-turning actions, and testing how the force needed changes with distance from the pivot. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
