    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 6 Seasons and adaptations, section 6.3, pages 149–154; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 93–97. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how plants and animals are adapted to hot, wet, cold or dry environments; use secondary information sources to research questions; group plants and animals with the environments they are adapted to. Key words: adaptation, thorn, waxy, burrow, gills, fin, resin, thaw, gland.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping living things in the three 3D lands, finishing the book's hot-and-dry sentences, or matching a fir tree's features to their reasons. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the three lands' panel only move the camera; finding means tapping the thing itself on the stage. Watch out: a cactus keeps its water in a fat stem, not in its thorns, and a fir tree's waxy needles and its thick resin bark help for different reasons.
