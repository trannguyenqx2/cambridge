    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 5 Forces and magnetism, section 5.5, pages 123–127; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 75–78. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: tell a magnet from a magnetic material; predict which materials are magnetic; plan and carry out a fair test; record results in a table; describe patterns in the results, including a result that does not fit; and make a conclusion. Key words: magnet, magnetic material, attract, repel, pole, alloy, predict, fair test.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D test bench. The fair test in this lesson always uses the same magnet held at the same distance, one object at a time — only the object changes. Watch out for the two tricky ideas in this section: the nickel coin is magnetic even though it contains no iron, and two magnetic materials (a nail and a paperclip) do NOT attract each other — only a magnet attracts them. 'Show me how' only demonstrates; it never counts as done.
