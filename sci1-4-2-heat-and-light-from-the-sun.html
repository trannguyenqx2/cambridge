    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 4 The Earth, section 4.2, pages 66–70; Workbook 1, pages 40–42. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about the Sun as a star; talk about the Sun as a source of heat and light. Key words: star, Sun, heat, light, sunlight, freeze.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D garden — tapping stars, keeping eyes safe, moving a cloud, growing plants, moving a thermometer, turning the Sun off. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The safety step follows the book: never look at the Sun, not even through sunglasses.
