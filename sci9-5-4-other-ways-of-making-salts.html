    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 5 Reactivity, section 5.4 Other ways of making salts, pages 178–183; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 95–100. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives (from the book): prepare a salt by using an acid and a carbonate; prepare a salt using neutralisation; do risk assessments for practical work; use word and symbol equations. Key words: bases, crystallise, erodes, limestone, neutralisation.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the models on the 3D chemistry bench itself, or sorting cards into groups. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done.
