    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 5 Properties of materials, section 5.4, pages 163–166; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 100–104. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: use what you know about mixtures to separate them; choose apparatus to carry out a practical task; carry out practical work in a safe way. Key words: condenser, conical flask, filter funnel, filter paper.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
