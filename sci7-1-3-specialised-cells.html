    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 1 Cells, section 1.3 Specialised cells, pages 11–15; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 7–10. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about some specialised animal and plant cells; explain how the structure of these specialised cells helps them to carry out their functions. Key words: absorb, adapted, axon, capillary, cilia, ciliated cell, dendrite, function, haemoglobin, mucus, neurone, palisade cell, pigment, red blood cell, root hair cell, specialised.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the cell models on the 3D stage itself, or sorting cards into the book's summary-table columns. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the stage' panel only move the camera; meeting a model means tapping it on the stage. The examples follow the book's order: the Getting started cell parts, then the three specialised animal cells (red blood cell, neurone, ciliated cell) with the book's structure-and-function table activity, then the two specialised plant cells (root hair cell, palisade cell) with their own table activity, and the 'I can…' self-check at the end.
