    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 9 Magnetism, section 9.3, pages 305–310; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 194–197. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how to make an electromagnet; recall some applications of electromagnets. Key words: coil, core, electromagnet, magnetic, magnetised, permanent magnets.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D lab bench — sorting magnetic from non-magnetic metals, building the book’s electromagnet (Activity 9.3.1: coil, iron-nail core, cell, switch), switching it on to pick up steel paperclips, testing its poles with a compass and a bar magnet, reversing its poles, and sorting jobs between electromagnets and permanent magnets. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Left out of the interactive lesson, with reasons: the Learner’s Book ‘draw the circuit diagram’ question (a drawing cannot be machine-checked — its parts are built for real in step 2 instead); the longer descriptive passages on MRI scanners, toasters and electric motors (condensed into the application sort cards and exercise cards, keeping the fire door, scrapyard sorter, toaster and electric bell); and the book’s self-assessment questions (folded into the ‘I can…’ lines at the end of the last step). Note: the Workbook prints its first 9.3 exercise with the number ‘6.3A’ — treated here as 9.3A, matching its content.
