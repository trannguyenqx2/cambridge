    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.2, pages 71–77; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 42–46. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: understand what is meant by speed; learn about the unit of speed; be able to calculate speed, distance and time. Key words: average speed, calculate, constant, m/s, metres, per, second, speed.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D track — the bus, triangle, aeroplane and investigation calculations all use the book values exactly. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
