    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 1 Respiration, section 1.4, pages 19–24; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 13–15. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: explain how every living cell gets the energy it needs; investigate how respiration releases some energy as heat; explain the difference between breathing and respiration. Key words: glucose, mitochondria (plus aerobic respiration, reactants, products, breathing).

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping things on the 3D bench, sorting the equation cards, or measuring the pea flasks. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The pea-flask temperatures (both flasks start at 18 °C; the live flask reaches 23 °C) are illustrative simulation values: the book's investigation asks learners to record their own measurements over time and plot them as a line graph. Excluded from the interactive steps: the thermogram discussion and the group poster/presentation activity from the book are covered in the conclusions and exercises instead (a photograph discussion and a group presentation cannot be machine-checked); the self-assessment checklist is reflected in the final step's 'I can…' conclusion.
