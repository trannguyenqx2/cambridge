    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 4 Grouping and identifying organisms, section 4.4, pages 132–136; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 82–86. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: use a key to identify an organism, or to classify it into a group; change a key from one style to a different style. Key word: dichotomous key.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D identification table or with statement choices. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
