    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 1 Cells, section 1.4 Cells, tissues and organs, pages 16–21; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 11–15. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about tissues, organs and organ systems in living organisms; recognise and name human organs that are part of different organ systems; locate and identify plant cells in the layers of a leaf. Key words: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism, epidermis, palisade layer, spongy layer, ciliated epithelium, onion epidermis.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping organs, tissues and leaf layers on the 3D bench, or sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the bench' panel only move the camera; finding means tapping the part itself. The steps follow the book's order: the Getting started body outline, the two example tissues (onion epidermis and ciliated epithelium), the leaf as an organ with its four layers, plant organs, and finally organs working together in systems. The group timeline project at the end of the book section is a classroom research task and is not part of this interactive lesson.
