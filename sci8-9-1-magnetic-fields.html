    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 9 Magnetism, section 9.1, pages 292–298; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 185–187. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe a magnetic field; understand that magnetic fields surround magnets; understand how magnetic fields interact. Key words: compass, like poles, magnet, magnetic, magnetic field, magnetic field lines.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D magnet bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
