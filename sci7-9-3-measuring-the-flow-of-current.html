    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 9 Electricity, section 9.3, pages 298–303; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 170–171. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn the unit for measuring current; learn how to measure current; learn a rule about current in series circuits. Key words: amps, in series.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
