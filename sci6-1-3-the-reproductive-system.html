    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021, ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 1 The human body, section 1.3 The reproductive system, pages 19–23; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 10–13. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe body changes that happen during puberty; name the parts of the reproductive system. Key words: fertilisation, hormones, menstruation, ova, puberty, reproductive system, sperm.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the sex cells and the male and female parts on the 3D stage, sorting puberty changes, and choosing what happens in menstruation. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The Workbook Challenge graph (how many girls started puberty at each age) is not reproduced because its bar values are pictures, not text, in the source file; the Challenge exercises use the book's mass table, whose values are exact.
