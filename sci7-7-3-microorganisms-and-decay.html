    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 7 Microorganisms in the environment, section 7.3, pages 238–242; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 135–141. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about microorganisms and decay; investigate how temperature affects the rate of decay; plan an experiment to test a hypothesis about decay. Key words: decay, decomposer, mould, organic matter, rot. The lesson opens like the book's Getting started: complete the sentence “A microorganism is …” in three different ways and discuss.

    How it works: the steps follow the book's order — decomposers and the mouldy apple, Activity 7.3.1 (which materials are organic matter), the temperature investigation with two bread slices treated identically, conclusions drawn from the recorded results, and finally planning the moisture experiment (change the water, measure the mould, keep everything else the same). Numbers in the investigation are the lesson's simulation values: each day the warm slice gains about 20% mould cover and the fridge slice about 4%. Workbook Challenge means were checked from the book's table: (14+18)/2 = 16.0 mm² at 40 °C ignoring the anomalous 5 mm², and (10+12+13)/3 = 11.7 mm² at 60 °C.
