    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.3, pages 85–90; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 53–55. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about what keeps the planets in orbit around the Sun; understand why planets move at different speeds; discover why objects moving in space do not slow down as they do on Earth. Key words: air resistance, circular, speed, vacuum.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D space scene, with sorting cards or choice questions. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
