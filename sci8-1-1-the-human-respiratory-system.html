    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 1 Respiration, section 1.1, pages 2–5; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 2–3. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn the names of the different parts of the human respiratory system; observe carefully, and record observations, as the structure of lungs is demonstrated.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the models on the 3D stage itself, sorting the pathway cards, or choosing what makes sound.
