    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 9 Electricity, section 9.5, pages 311–321; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 174–176. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: find out what happens to the current when you add more cells in a circuit, and when you add more lamps in a circuit. Key words: adding components, dimmer, position, removing components.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D circuit bench, with sorting cards or choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
