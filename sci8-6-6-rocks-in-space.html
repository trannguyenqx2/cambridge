    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.6, pages 218–229; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 130–133. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: discover that asteroids are rocks that are smaller than planets; know that scientists believe asteroids to be rocks left over from the formation of the Solar System. Key words: asteroid belt, asteroids, craters, impacts.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D space stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
