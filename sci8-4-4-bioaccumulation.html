    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 4 Ecosystems, section 4.4, pages 137–142 (unit Check your progress and Project follow on pages 143–146); Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 82–87. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about DDT; use a model to explain what happens to DDT in a food chain; explain what bioaccumulation is and why it happens. Key words: accumulate, bioaccumulation, biomagnification, insecticide, persistent, toxic.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D field and pond. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
