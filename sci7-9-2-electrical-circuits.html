    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 9 Electricity, section 9.2, pages 293–297; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 168–169. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn how to draw and compare circuit diagrams; learn the circuit symbols for cells, switches, lamps, buzzers and ammeters. Key words: ammeter, circuit diagram, circuit symbols.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench, with the sort widget or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
