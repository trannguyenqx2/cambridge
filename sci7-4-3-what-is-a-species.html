    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 4 Grouping and identifying organisms, section 4.3, pages 128–131; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 77–81. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: look carefully for similarities and differences between organisms; compare two different species; find out how scientists decide if two organisms belong to the same or different species. Key words: species, fertile, infertile, offspring, identical, variation, specimen.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D reserve or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
