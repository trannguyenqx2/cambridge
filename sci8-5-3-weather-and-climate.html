    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 5 Materials and their structure, section 5.3, pages 160–165; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 97–102. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn the difference between weather and climate; make observations of the weather. Key words: atmosphere, climate, climatology, humidity, meteorology, statistics, visibility, weather.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action at the 3D weather station — finding the six features that describe the weather, setting up fair daily readings (shade, same time, results table), reading the Workbook's Reykjavik temperatures for 26 July (13 °C at 00.00, 16 °C at 06.00, 18 °C at 12.00, 19 °C at 18.00 — the book's own table values, with the week's maximum of 20 °C on 30 July), judging whether a weather source might be biased, sorting short-term weather from long-term climate, and visiting four climate zones. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
