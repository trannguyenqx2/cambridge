    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 8 Changes to materials, section 8.4, pages 275–287; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 163–165. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about different ways of telling if a chemical reaction has happened; test gases given off in reactions so that you can identify them; carry out practical tasks safely. Key words: cloudy, glowing, precipitate.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
