    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.1, pages 62–70; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 38–41. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: understand what is meant by balanced and unbalanced forces; describe the effects of balanced forces on motion; describe the effects of unbalanced forces on motion. Key words: force, balanced, unbalanced, opposite, direction, speed up, slow down, change direction, analogy.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D force park — finding the balanced forces on a still rock, feeling wind against friction, pushing the rock with a vehicle, opening a parachute, hitting a tennis ball, running the two-newton-meter investigation from the book, and sorting the book’s seven situations into balanced and unbalanced. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done.
