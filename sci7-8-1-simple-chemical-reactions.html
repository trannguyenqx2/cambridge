    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 8 Changes to materials, section 8.1, pages 253–261; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 144–148. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about the chemical properties of some metals; learn to recognise that a chemical reaction has taken place. Key words: chemical reaction, combine, product, react, reactant.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards and choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
