    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 2 Materials: properties and changes, section 2.4 Chemical reactions, pages 56–62; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 33–36. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book. The section's closing project (Electrical insulators) and the unit check-your-progress pages are classroom research and review tasks and are not part of this interactive lesson.

    Objectives: find out that in a chemical reaction, substances react together to form new substances; identify reactants and products in chemical reactions; observe and describe evidence for chemical reactions; record observations in drawings; write a conclusion for an investigation; measure temperature. Key words: chemical reaction, react, reactants, products, evidence.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the equipment on the 3D lab bench itself, or sorting cards into groups. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the bench' panel only move the camera; doing an experiment means tapping the equipment on the bench. The experiments follow the book's order: the candle and the spoon held over its flame, vinegar with bicarbonate of soda, iodine with cornstarch, breathing into limewater, and wire wool in vinegar with a thermometer. The book asks learners to measure their own temperatures, so the thermometer readings here (20 °C rising to 28 °C) are the simulation's own example values.
