    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 9 Magnetism, section 9.2, pages 299–304; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 188–193. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: discover that the Earth has a magnetic field; learn that the core of the Earth acts as a magnet. Key words: geographic north pole, magnetic north, naturally occurring, navigate.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
