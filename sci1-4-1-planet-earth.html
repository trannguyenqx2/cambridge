    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 4 The Earth, section 4.1, pages 61–65; Workbook 1, pages 37–39. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about planet Earth and the Sun; learn that parts of Earth's surface are land and that water covers some parts of the surface; write a little science report. Key words: science report, sea, space, Sun, surface, astronaut, lake, land, Moon, orbit.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D space corner — tapping the Earth ball, spinning the globe, flying to the rocket window, counting the planets in order, sorting living things and finishing a science report with word tiles. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Walking on the ground we cannot see Earth's ball shape because Earth is so big; astronaut Meg can see the whole ball from space.
