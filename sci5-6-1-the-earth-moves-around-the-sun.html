    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 6 Seasons and adaptations, section 6.1, pages 136–141; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 84–87. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe the orbit of the Earth around the Sun; describe how the tilt of the Earth creates different seasons in different places; use diagrams as models of the orbit and the seasons, knowing a diagram shows some important features but not everything. Key words: orbit, ellipse, slight ellipse, revolve, rotate, axis, tilt, hemisphere, equator, season, year.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the Sun and the tilted Earth globes on the orbit model, sorting ideas, and reading the season from the Earth's position. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
