    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 4 The Earth, section 4.4, pages 79–83; Workbook 1, pages 47–49. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: see that the land is made of rocks and soil; understand that soil contains living things; make predictions. Key words: soil, rock, earthworm, dig, roots, magnifying glass.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action. Your child digs the bed with the trowel (the dug soil collects in the tray), hunts for six things in the magnified soil view, sorts the finds into the three parts of soil, watches the earthworm mix the soil and builds the worm–soil–plant–food chain, then investigates two soils — park soil has 2 living things, garden soil has 5 — predicts which is better for plants, and finishes with the Workbook's carrot true-or-not statements. Safety point from the book: wash your hands after touching soil. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done, and every answer stays locked until the action itself is done.
