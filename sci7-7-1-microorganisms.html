    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 7 Microorganisms in the environment, section 7.1, pages 227–231; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 127–130. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about the different kinds of microorganism, and grow some microorganisms on agar jelly. Key words: agar jelly, algae, bacteria, colony, fungi, microorganism, mushroom, Petri dish, protozoa, single-celled, sterile, toadstool, yeast.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
