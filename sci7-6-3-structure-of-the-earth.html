    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 6 Earth physics, section 6.3, pages 205-209; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 120-121. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe a model of the structure of the Earth; understand how the continents on Earth have changed. Key words: continental, core, crust, drift, magma, mantle, molten, tectonic plates.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D model bench. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. Show me how only demonstrates - it never counts as done.
