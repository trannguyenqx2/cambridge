    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 4, section 4.5, pages 137–140; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 87–89. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn how to create your own key; learn how to change your key following feedback. Key words: key, identify, feature, pair of statements, species, feedback.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
