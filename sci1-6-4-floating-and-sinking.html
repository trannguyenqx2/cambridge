    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 6 Forces, section 6.4, pages 128–132; Workbook 1, pages 74–76. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: see that some objects float and others sink; sort and group objects; record observations in a table; make predictions. Key words: float, sink, predict, test, air, clay, shape, water.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action. In step 1 your child predicts for six things — a prediction is a guess, so it cannot be wrong yet. In step 2 they tap each thing beside the tank to drop it in and see what really happens; the record table in panel 3 fills in by itself. Later steps test a clay ball, change the clay's shape until it floats, and test things with air inside. The dark 'Show me' line checks the action itself, and answers stay locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in panel 1 only move the camera to look. Safety note from the book: always be careful around deep water.
