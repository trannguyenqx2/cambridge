    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 7 Diet and growth, section 7.2, pages 238–244; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 138–143. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out what is meant by a balanced diet; think about the nutrients you should try to eat each day; learn why you should try not to eat too much of some nutrients. Key words: balanced diet, constipation, fibre.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D kitchen stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
