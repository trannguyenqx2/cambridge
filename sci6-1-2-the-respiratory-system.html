    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021, ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 1 The human body, section 1.2, pages 12–18; Workbook 6 (2nd edition, 2021, ISBN 9781108742986), pages 6–9. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how the respiratory system works; make and explain a model of breathing; show that breathing has two stages, breathing in and breathing out; measure breathing rate; do practical work safely; record results in a table; draw a line graph of results; use results to make a conclusion; find information to answer a scientific question. Key words: breathing, breathing rate, diaphragm, lungs, windpipe.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the parts of the respiratory system on the body model, feeling how the chest moves, sorting what happens when we breathe in and out, working the bottle model of breathing, and investigating breathing rate, in the same order as the book. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
