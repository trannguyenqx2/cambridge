    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 2 Materials and their structure, section 2.5, pages 45–50; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 32–33. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn what an atom and an element are; find out about different atoms and elements; learn about the Periodic Table; use symbols to represent the names of elements. Key words: atom, element, group, metals, nanotube, non-metal, period, symbol, Periodic Table.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
