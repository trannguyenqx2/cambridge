    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 5 Humans, section 5.2, pages 92–95; Workbook 1, pages 54–57. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: list all five senses and what each one does; match every sense with the body part used for it; observe things with more than one sense. Key words: senses, sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch, eye, ear, nose, mouth, skin, predict.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping things in the 3D garden itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The lesson follows the book: first the five senses and their body parts, then Sofia's blindfold game from the book (with her eyes covered, she has to rely on listening to Zara), then the smell-pot investigation — predict first, record in the table, then open the pots and check, like Arun in the book. The pots always hide the same things: pot A is coffee, pot B is lemon, pot C is chocolate. No real sound, smell or taste is ever needed: hearing is shown with sound-wave rings, smell with drifting scent wisps, taste and touch with pictures.
