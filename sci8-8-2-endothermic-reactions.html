    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 8 Chemical reactions, section 8.2, pages 267–273; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 167–169. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about chemical reactions that absorb energy; distinguish between exothermic and endothermic reactions and processes; learn about the use of exothermic and endothermic reactions and processes. Key words: endothermic process, endothermic reactions.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D lab bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
