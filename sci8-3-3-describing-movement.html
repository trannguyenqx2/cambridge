    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.3, pages 78–84; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 47–50. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn how to use graphs to describe movement; understand what a distance–time graph shows; learn to draw a distance–time graph. Key words: distance–time graph, stationary, at rest, sketch, safety precautions, constant speed.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D movement course — walking at a constant speed, reading the four sections of the car journey, calculating train speeds from graph values, comparing sketch lines, describing Marcus’s ride, and planning the walking-and-running investigation. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark Show me line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. Show me how only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
