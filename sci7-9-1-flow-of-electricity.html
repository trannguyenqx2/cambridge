    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 9 Electricity, section 9.1, pages 288–292; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 166–167. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: understand how electricity flows around a circuit; learn about the electrons that flow to make electric current. Key words: attract, battery, cell, components, current, electrons, free to move, negative charge, repel, terminals.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D circuit bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
