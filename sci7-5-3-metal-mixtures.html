    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 5 Properties of materials, section 5.3, pages 155–162; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 98–99. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about metal mixtures (alloys); use particle theory to explain the differences in the properties of metals and their alloys. Key words: alloy, bronze, disrupt, steel.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
