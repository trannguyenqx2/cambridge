    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 5 Properties of materials, section 5.6, pages 173–185; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 107–110. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn how to tell an acid from an alkali; learn how to make and use indicators; use the pH scale to find out more about acids and alkalis. Key words: indicator, litmus, neutral, pH scale, universal indicator.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
