    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.5, pages 99–104; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 60–63. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: find out what energy is; learn about different energy stores and transfers; discover how some ways that energy can be stored more easily than others. Key words: chemical, elastic potential, electrical, energy, fuel, gravitational potential, joule, kinetic, light, luminous, sound, stored, thermal, transferred.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
