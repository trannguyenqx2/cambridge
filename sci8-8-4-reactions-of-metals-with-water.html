    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 8 Chemical reactions, section 8.4, pages 280–284; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 173–175. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe the reactions of some metals with water; carry out an investigation; compare how reactive some metals are with water. Key words: reactivity, sandpaper.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
