    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 5 Properties of materials, section 5.5, pages 167–172; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 105–106. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about the properties of acids and alkalis; learn how to work safely with acids and alkalis; find out about hazard symbols. Key words: acid, alkalis, corrosive, flammable, harmful, irritate, oxidising, toxic.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
