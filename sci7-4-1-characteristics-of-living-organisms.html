    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 4 Grouping and identifying organisms, section 4.1, pages 121–123; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 71–74. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: think about what makes living organisms different from non-living things, and learn about the seven characteristics of living organisms. Key words: excretion, growth, movement, nutrition, organism, reproduction, respiration, sensitivity.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
