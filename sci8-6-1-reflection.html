    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.1, pages 185–191; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 111–113. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how light is reflected from a plane surface; understand the law of reflection; be able to draw ray diagrams to show reflection of light. Key words: angle of incidence, angle of reflection, incident ray, law of reflection, normal, perpendicular, plane mirror, protractor, ray diagram, rays, reflection, set square.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D optics bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
