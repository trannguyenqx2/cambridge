    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 1 Living things, section 1.3, pages 9–11; Workbook 1, pages 8–10. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out if plants need light; carry out an investigation and make a prediction. Key words: investigation, light, question, answer, asks, grow, dark — the book's own key words for section 1.3.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D scene — tapping the window light, covering Plant B with the box, waiting five days, then choosing the words that match each plant. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The five 'days' pass in a few seconds here; with real plants, Marcus's investigation takes about a week, and the plant in the dark really does grow tall, thin and yellow.
