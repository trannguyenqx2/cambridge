    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 3 Materials in my world, section 3.3, pages 48–52; Workbook 1, pages 31–33. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: sort materials into groups; test materials to find out their properties; ask questions about the properties of materials; say how recycling helps planet Earth. Key words: sort, group, material, recycle, waste, Earth, planet.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — sorting things into trays on the 3D table, ticking the spoon's groups, or putting waste into the right recycling bin. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Two ideas to watch: the same material set can be sorted in different ways (by material, by hard/soft, by rough/smooth), and one object — the spoon — belongs to several groups at once. Sorting in steps 1–3 is by observing; the book also contrasts observing with testing, which the final 'I can…' step names. Safety point from the book: some metal things can be sharp, so touch them carefully.
