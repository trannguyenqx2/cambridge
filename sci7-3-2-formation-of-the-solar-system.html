    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.2, pages 79–84; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 49–52. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn how scientists think the Solar System was formed; think about objects in space growing larger and increasing in mass; understand that as their mass increases, their gravity increases — and that stronger gravity can attract even more mass. Key words: axis, contradict, evidence, formed, model, nebula, observe, orbit, plane, spin, support.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D space scene or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
