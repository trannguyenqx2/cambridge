    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 4 The Earth, section 4.3 Rocks, pages 73–77; Workbook 1, pages 44–46. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: say that water covers most of the Earth and that land is made of rock and soil; look closely at rocks, sort them, and make predictions, then check them. Key words: rock, stone, sand, quarry, hard, soft, scratch.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D rock garden — tapping rocks, looking at sand grains up close, scratching rocks with a nail and with a wooden stick, and sorting the six named rocks by colour. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line) and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the key ideas: a stone is just a small rock, sand is made of tiny pieces of rock, and scratching tells us which rocks are hard and which are soft.
