    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.7, pages 108–119; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 64–68. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how the random movement of particles causes diffusion; understand how diffusion happens in liquids and in gases; understand that the speed of diffusion depends on the concentration difference and on temperature. Key words: diffusion, concentration.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench — opening the tap between two gas jars, checking the concentrations on both sides, adding ink to water with a pipette, sorting real cases of diffusion, timing diffusion in cold, warm and hot water, and following a smell and a concentrated juice as they spread. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done.
