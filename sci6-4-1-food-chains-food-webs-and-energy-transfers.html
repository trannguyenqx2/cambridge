    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 4 Food chains and food webs, section 4.1, pages 97–103; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 51–54. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: draw food chains; explain a food web and identify food chains in a food web; explain how a food web shows feeding relationships in nature; identify the source of energy in food chains and food webs; describe how energy is transferred in food chains and food webs. Key words: accurate, food chain, food web, represent — with producer, consumer, herbivore, carnivore and omnivore from the Getting started activity.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping a producer and a consumer, sorting consumers into herbivores, carnivores and omnivores, building the book's cabbage food chain link by link, reading the rainforest food web, following the book's bean plant energy chain from the Sun to the hawk, then sorting where a consumer's energy goes. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the key ideas of this section: arrows in a food chain point the way the food — and the energy — moves, and the energy passed on gets less at every link because some is used, stored or lost as heat.
