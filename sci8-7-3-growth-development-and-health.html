    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 7 Diet and growth, section 7.3, pages 245–248; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 144–152. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn how growth takes place; find out the difference between growth and development; think about how diet and exercise affect growth, development and health; learn how smoking affects health. Key words: carbon monoxide, development, embryo, nicotine, particulates, tar.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
