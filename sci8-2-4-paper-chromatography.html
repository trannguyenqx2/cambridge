    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 2 Properties of materials, section 2.4, pages 51–61; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 33–37. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: use paper chromatography to separate dissolved substances; interpret chromatograms; use scientific language accurately. Key words: paper chromatography, chromatogram, solvent front, permanent, solvent, soluble.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench — tapping pens, setting up the paper, running the solvent, reading the separated bands, sorting dye results and choosing a solvent for leaf pigment. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
