    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 5 Forces and electricity, section 5.2, pages 119–123; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 62–67. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe the effects of different forces on an object at rest and in motion; use force diagrams to show the name, size and direction of forces; practise observing over time. Key words: force, applied force, gravity, friction, at rest, direction, speed, force diagram.

    How it works: the six steps replay the book's ping pong ball investigation on a 3D table — the ball at rest, blowing through a straw, rolling into a book, rolling over a cloth, flicking it back with a bat, and pressing it. Each step checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line) and the conclusion stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. The Workbook exercises (Focus, Practice, Challenge) include the book's force-diagram task for a ball resting on the ground. The Workbook's picture-only soccer items are reworded as short written scenarios, because the source text pack carries no pictures.
