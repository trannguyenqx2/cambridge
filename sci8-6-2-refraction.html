    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.2, pages 192–198; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 114–117. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe how light is refracted at the boundary between air and glass or air and water; describe how light changes speed when it passes between different substances; draw ray diagrams to show how light is refracted. Key words: angle of refraction, away from the normal, bent, distorted, lenses, medium, refraction, towards the normal.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D optics bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
