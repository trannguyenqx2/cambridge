    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 1 Living things, section 1.4, pages 12–15; Workbook 1, pages 11–14. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn how plants need water; predict what will happen in an investigation; record observations in tables; say if what happened was what we predicted. Key words: water, roots, predict, record, table, explain.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. In Step 2 the learner predicts first, then runs the book's leaves-versus-roots test; in Step 3 they run the five-day watering investigation and record the heights (in bricks) in the table on the right, exactly like the book's results table: water every day / water two times a week / no water. Watch out for the two big ideas: plants drink through their roots, and too much water can hurt a plant just as no water can.
