    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.4, pages 91–98; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 56–59. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out what tides are; learn about tidal forces and where they come from; discover how tidal forces affect the oceans and the land. Key words: coastal, depth, earthquake, earth tide, force of attraction, harbour, tidal force, tidal range, tide.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D Earth–Moon model. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
