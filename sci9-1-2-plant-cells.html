    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 1 Photosynthesis and the carbon cycle, section 1.2, pages 7–17; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 9–16. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out where photosynthesis happens in plants; learn why plants need magnesium and nitrate. Key words: fertiliser, stomata (singular: stoma), yield — plus chloroplast, chlorophyll and starch from the section text.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the plant, the cell model and the test equipment in the 3D leaf lab, sorting the mineral jobs, and reading Sofia's duckweed results (Workbook 1.2A: dishes with 0–4 grains of fertiliser ended with 5, 9, 10, 8 and 0 plants). The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the leaf lab' panel only move the camera; finding means tapping the thing itself. The starch test follows the book's method and safety order: boil the leaf, switch the flame OFF before ethanol, warm the ethanol in hot water to take the green colour out, soften the leaf, then add iodine — blue-black shows starch.
