    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 5 Humans, section 5.3, pages 96–98; Workbook 1, pages 58–60. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out how humans are similar and how humans are different; look carefully and make a drawing to show what we observe; sort humans into groups. Key words: blonde, same, twins.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping the children in the 3D school yard itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the school yard' panel only move the camera to look at a child. Heights are real numbers used all through the lesson and the exercises (Leo 106 cm is the shortest, Ben 130 cm is the tallest), and Tom and Tim the twins are identical on purpose.
