    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.6, pages 105–110; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 64–66. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn about energy changing; discover that energy changes when something happens; give examples of changes in energy. Key words: change, event, process.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
