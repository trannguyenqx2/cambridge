    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 1 Living things, section 1.1, pages 2–4; Workbook 1, pages 2–4. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find living things and things that have never been alive; put things into groups. Key words: alive, living, non-living, animal, plant, group, look, water.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping things in the 3D garden itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the garden' panel only move the camera to look at a thing; finding means tapping the thing in the garden. Watch out for the two tricky ideas in this section: water can move but is not alive, and a wooden chair is not alive now but the wood came from a living tree.
