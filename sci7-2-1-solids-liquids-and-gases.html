    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 2 Materials and their structure, section 2.1, pages 22–28; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 16–20. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives (the book's 'We are going to…'): sort the states of matter into solids, liquids and gases; learn about the properties of solids, liquids and gases; use particle theory to describe and explain the structure and properties of solids, liquids and gases. Key words: compressed, flow, hypothesis, matter, particle, pour, property, states of matter, theory, vacuum, vibrate, volume.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
