    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 7 Diet and growth, section 7.4, pages 249–259; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 153–159. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about hinge joints and ball-and-socket joints; find out how a pair of muscles moves the arm at the elbow. Key words: antagonistic muscles, ball-and-socket joints, biceps, contraction, exoskeleton, hinge joints, joints, muscles, relax, skeleton, tendons, triceps.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D body-lab stage — sorting protection bones from movement bones (the book’s Getting started), identifying hinge and ball-and-socket joints (Activity 7.4.1), naming the three arm bones, bending the elbow with the biceps while feeling the muscle work (Activity 7.4.2), straightening it with the triceps, and testing the book’s model arm (the Think like a scientist investigation) at all four hooks. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Three book items were adapted or left out: the model-arm forces (12 N, 6 N, 4 N and 3 N at 10, 20, 30 and 40 cm for one fixed load) are the simulation’s own readings, worked out from moments — the book gives the method and the hook spacing but no results, so no book number was invented; the Workbook 7.4A finger readings could not be recovered exactly from the extracted scale pictures, so the Focus exercise uses self-checking scale-reading cards in the same style instead of Arun’s own table; and the Workbook 7.4C task of writing your own full investigation plan and results chart is an open writing task that cannot be machine-checked, so only its testable-hypothesis choice is practised here. The unit Check your progress questions and the Mars project belong to the whole of Unit 7, not to section 7.4, and are not included.
