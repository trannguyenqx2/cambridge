    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 8 Changes to materials, section 8.2, pages 262–269; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 149–153. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn how to make a neutral solution; learn why neutralisation is important. Key words: burette, decay, digest, filtrate, indigestion, neutralisation, neutralised.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
