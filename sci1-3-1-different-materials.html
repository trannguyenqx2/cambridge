    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 3 Materials in my world, section 3.1, pages 41–43; Workbook 1, pages 23–25. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: look at things carefully and use your hands to find out what materials they are made of; draw things and write the names of their materials. Key words: fabric, feel, glass, materials, metal, paper, plastic, rock, rubber, wood.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping things in the 3D playground itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the playground' panel only move the camera to look at a thing; finding a material means tapping the thing itself in the playground. Two things in the playground are made of plastic (the steps and the bucket), so eight materials are hidden in nine things.
