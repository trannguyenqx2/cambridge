    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner’s Book 9 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 2 Properties of materials, section 2.1, pages 42–46; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 24–26. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn more about the structure of the atom, and learn how the structure of the Periodic Table is related to the structure of the elements. Key words: atomic number, concentric, density, electron shells, electronic structure, electrostatic forces, energy levels, highest energy level, mass number, outermost electron shell, Periodic Table.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the stage — tapping parts of the atom model, reading element tiles, comparing two 1 cm³ samples (the book’s own values: sodium 0.97 g, iron 7.87 g), tapping the electron shells, and sorting cards by electronic structure and by the pieces of the book’s card model atom (Activity 2.1.1). The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in panel 1 only move the camera; a thing counts only when it is tapped on the stage itself.
