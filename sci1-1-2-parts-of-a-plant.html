    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 1 Living things, section 1.2, pages 5–8; Workbook 1, pages 5–7. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about the parts of plants; name the parts of plants; draw parts of a plant. Key words: flower, leaf, stem, root, seeds, observe, parts, model, magnifying glass, similar, different.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping the parts of the big plant in the 3D garden itself. The front of the soil bed is see-through, so the roots can be observed under the ground. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. In Step 4 the learner must tap the magnifying glass first, then observe the three different plants — they look different, but they all have the same parts.
