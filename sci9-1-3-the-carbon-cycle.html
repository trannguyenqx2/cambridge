    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 1 Photosynthesis and the carbon cycle, section 1.3 The carbon cycle, pages 18–27; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 17–19. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out how carbon atoms move between the air, living organisms and fossil fuels; bring together respiration and photosynthesis to explain how they affect the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere. Key words: carbon, compound, carbohydrate, photosynthesis, respiration, feeding, decomposition, fossil fuels, combustion, hydrogencarbonate indicator.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the stations of the cycle in the 3D scene, or sorting cards for the indicator-tube investigation and the final review. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and each conclusion stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the key ideas in this section: a carbon atom stays a carbon atom as it moves round the cycle; plants respire all the time as well as photosynthesise in the light; and fossil fuels are not fossils — they are the changed remains of organisms, burned by combustion.
