    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 5 Materials and their structure, section 5.5, pages 172–184; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 106–110. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about the atmosphere of the Earth; learn how a change in the atmosphere can affect the climate; learn about renewable resources. Key words: analogy, biodegradable, bioplastics, deforestation, emissions, fossil fuels, global warming, greenhouse effect, locked up, photosynthesis, recycled, renewable, resources.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D Earth-and-air stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
