    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner’s Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 5 Materials and their structure, section 5.2, pages 152–159; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 91–96. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: explain what is meant by purity; calculate percentage purity; describe why it is difficult to get a pure product. Key words: carat, translucent, suggest — plus purity, pure, impurity and mixture.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D laboratory bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark ‘Show me’ line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
