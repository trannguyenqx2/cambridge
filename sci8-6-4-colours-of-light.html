    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.4, pages 204–211; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 122–124. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: discover what happens when colours of light are added; discover what happens when colours of light are subtracted; discover why we see different colours. Key words: absorbed, coloured filters, cyan, magenta, non-luminous, primary colours, subtraction, transmit.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D light lab. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
