    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 4 Maintaining life, section 4.2 Transpiration, pages 140–146; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 72–74. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives (from the book): find out how water vapour is lost from plant leaves; summarise why plants need water. Key words: transpiration, wilted.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the models on the 3D bench itself, or sorting cards into groups.
