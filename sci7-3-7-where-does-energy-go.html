    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.7, pages 111–120; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 67–70. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: discover that when energy is changed, some of it may be wasted; learn that some of this energy can never be recovered. Key words: dissipated, recovered, useful, wasted energy.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
