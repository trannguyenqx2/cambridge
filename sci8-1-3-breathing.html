    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition), ISBN 9781108742825, Unit 1 Respiration, section 1.3 Breathing, pages 13–18; Workbook 8 (2nd edition), ISBN 9781108742856, pages 6–12. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: measure how much air you can push out of your lungs in one breath; learn how the muscles between the ribs and the diaphragm move air into and out of the lungs. Key words: breathing, contract, relax — plus diaphragm, intercostal muscles, volume, pressure, measuring cylinder and mean.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action, in the book's order. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
