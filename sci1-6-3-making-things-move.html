    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 6 Forces, section 6.3, pages 121–127; Workbook 1, pages 71–73. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: explore pushes of different sizes (tiny, little and bigger); find the things that run on electricity; say what they think will happen before trying; write what they see in a table; and afterwards tell what happened and compare it with what they said. Key words: push, tiny, little, bigger, cell, mains.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action, and the answer stays locked until the lesson's own check (the dark 'Show me' line) passes. Steps 2 and 3 fill in the record table in panel 3 with the book's own results: the chair does not move with a tiny or a little balloon push but moves with a bigger push, and the pen slides 4 bricks with a little push, 6 bricks with a bigger push and 9 bricks with the biggest push — the numbers printed in each step are exactly the numbers the check looks for. 'Show me how' only demonstrates and never counts as done. Safety point from the book: children must never touch mains plugs and wires; in this lesson all mains machines are models on a table.
