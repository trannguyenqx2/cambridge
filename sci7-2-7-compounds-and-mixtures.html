    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 2 Materials and their structure, section 2.7, pages 58–68; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 38–41. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: make a compound and a mixture; learn about the difference between a compound and a mixture; give examples of mixtures. Key words: composition, evaporating basin, filings, mineral, mixture, natural emissions, pipe-clay triangle, pure.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
