    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 4 Ecosystems, section 4.2, pages 127–133; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 72–75. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about some of the many different kinds of ecosystem on Earth; describe some of the different habitats in an ecosystem. Key words: ecosystem, habitat, environment, decomposer, producer, predator.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D field site — showing what makes an ecosystem more than a place, visiting a mangrove forest, Arctic sea ice and a rice paddy, choosing investigation tools, and sorting the habitats of a garden. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Fieldwork care from the book: stay with a partner, handle any animals gently, and release them exactly where you found them.
