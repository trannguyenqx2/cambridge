    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 6 Earth physics, section 6.2, pages 196–204; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 114–119. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: learn that sound waves can be reflected; discover what can happen when sound is reflected. Key words: echo, effect on the sound, property, reflected, unwanted.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench or with choice buttons. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
