    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 7, section 7.4, pages 243–252; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 142–143. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: draw and interpret food webs that include microorganisms as decomposers; think about how microorganisms contribute to food webs. Key words: decomposer, microorganism, nutrients, food web, producer, consumer.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
