    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 4 Ecosystems, section 4.1, pages 120–126; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 69–71. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about some of the animals and plants that live in a desert; think about how these animals and plants interact with each other; learn what an ecosystem is; think about some of the different habitats in a desert ecosystem. Key words: adaptations, ecology, ecosystem, environment, food web, habitat, interact, nectar, nocturnal, pollen, pollinating.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D desert — tapping the cacti, the nesting birds, the non-living factors, the pollinators, sorting the food web, and visiting habitats. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
