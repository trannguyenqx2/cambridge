    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.3, pages 199–203; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 118–121. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn how white light is made from many colours; discover how dispersion of white light can be done with a prism; recall the colours of white light in the correct order. Key words: dispersion, prism, spectrum, triangular.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D light bench. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
