    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 5 Forces and magnetism, section 5.1, pages 104–107; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 60–63. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: identify gravity and normal and applied forces; use force diagrams to show the name and direction of forces acting on an object. Key words: applied force, attracts, exert, force, gravity, normal force.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — dropping things so gravity pulls them down, tapping a book and its table to reveal the pair of arrows, completing a force diagram for a standing person, pulling a door and pushing a trolley, then sorting pushes, pulls and force types. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the key idea of this section: a resting book is NOT free of forces — gravity pulls it down and the table pushes it up with an equal normal force, so the two arrows on its force diagram are the same size.
