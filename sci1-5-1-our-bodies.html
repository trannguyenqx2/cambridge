    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 5 Humans, section 5.1, pages 88–91; Workbook 1, pages 50–53. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: name the main parts of the body; compare bodies (who is taller or shorter, whose feet are longer or shorter). Key words: body, human, label, long, short, skin, tall.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the named part on Sam, the big friendly figure, placing word labels on his body, or tapping the right child on the comparing platform and the measuring mats. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'Parts of the body' panel only move the camera to look at a part; answering means tapping the part itself in the scene. Comparing follows the book: Femi is the taller of the two boys, and the children's feet are measured in bricks, just like the Workbook challenge. One idea from the book to talk about: some people's bodies are different, and they can do just as many things as anyone else.
