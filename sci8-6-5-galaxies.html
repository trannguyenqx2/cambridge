    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 6 Light, section 6.5, pages 212–217; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 125–129. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: discover that galaxies contain dust, gas, stars and other solar systems; recall the three shapes of galaxies; recall that gravity holds a galaxy together; use a sample-and-scale method to estimate a number that is too large to count. Key words: solar system, planet, galaxy, moon, star, elliptical galaxy, irregular, spiral, stellar dust, universe.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D space stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
