    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 6 Forces, section 6.1, pages 108–114; Workbook 1, pages 64–66. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: observe how things around us move; talk about the ways in which things move; make predictions; measure how far things move. Key words: force, rolling, slope, jump, rope, swing, pull, run, turn.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping things in the 3D playground itself. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'In the playground' panel only move the camera to look at a thing; playing with a thing means tapping the thing itself. In the slope test the results are always the same — ball 5 bricks, tape roll 3 bricks, pencil 1 brick — so children can check their predictions against the table.
