    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 2 Properties of materials, section 2.3, pages 54–63; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 33–36. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: describe the structure of an ion and compare it with an atom; explain how ionic and covalent bonds are formed; explain what a molecule is; write the formulae of some ionic and covalent compounds. Key words: chemical bond, covalent bond, dot and cross diagram, ion, ionic bond, ionic compound, molecule, stable.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D atom models — inspecting atoms, making ions by losing or gaining electrons, joining ions into ionic compounds and sharing electrons into covalent molecules. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'Atom lab' panel only move the camera; the action itself must be done by tapping the model on the bench. Watch out: an ion is NOT the same as its atom — the electron numbers differ, so the charges no longer balance; and potassium loses its outer electron more easily than lithium because that electron is further from the nucleus and held less strongly.
