    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner’s Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 8 Changes to materials, section 8.3, pages 270–274; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 154–162. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: discuss questions that you can test; plan an investigation; think about what the results of an investigation tell you. Key words: remedy, variable.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D bench, with a choice question or sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. ‘Show me how’ only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
