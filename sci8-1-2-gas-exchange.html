    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, 2021, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 1 Respiration, section 1.2 Gas exchange, pages 6–12; Workbook 8 (2nd edition, 2021, ISBN 9781108742856), pages 4–5. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out how oxygen gets into the blood from the air and how carbon dioxide goes in the other direction; do an experiment to think about why the air sacs in the lungs need to be very small; do an experiment to compare how much carbon dioxide there is in inspired air and expired air. Key words: alveoli, analogy, capillaries, diffusion, expired air, gas exchange, haemoglobin, inspired air, limewater.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action, in the book's order. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
