    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 3 Forces and energy, section 3.1, pages 69–78; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 42–48. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: understand that the force of gravity acts between objects; learn what affects the strength of the force of gravity on an object; use the terms weight and mass correctly. Key words: gravity, weight, mass, contact force, newtons, kilograms, quantity, formula triangle, accurate.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D gravity lab or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
