    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 7 Diet and growth, section 7.1, pages 230–237; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), pages 134–137. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about the six types of nutrient that everyone needs to eat; find out why they are needed; learn about some good sources of these nutrients. Key words: anaemia, carbohydrate, fat, minerals, nutrients, oil, protein, starch, vitamin A, vitamin C, vitamin D, vitamins.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D kitchen stage. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
