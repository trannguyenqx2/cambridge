    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition; ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 1 Photosynthesis and the carbon cycle, section 1.1 Photosynthesis, pages 2–6; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 2–8. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: practise using the word equation for photosynthesis; learn what photosynthesis is and why it is important. Key words: chlorophyll, light intensity, photosynthesis.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the plants and apparatus on the 3D bench itself, or sorting cards into groups. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the bench' panel only move the camera; doing an experiment means tapping the apparatus on the bench. The steps follow the book's order: maize grown in light and in the dark, what a plant takes in, collecting the gas from pondweed and testing it with a glowing splint, then the lamp-distance investigation of light intensity. The book asks learners to count their own bubbles, so the bubble counts on the bench (24 per minute close to the lamp, 6 per minute far away) are the simulation's own example values. Workbook 1.1C asks learners to write their own hypothesis and plan; here that open writing task is practised as structured choices about the hypothesis's variables. The book's team game on words beginning with 'photo-' and its peer assessment of results charts are classroom discussion tasks and are not part of this interactive lesson.
