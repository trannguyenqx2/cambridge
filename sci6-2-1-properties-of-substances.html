    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 2 Materials: properties and changes, section 2.1, pages 35–41; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 19–22. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: understand that the temperature at which a substance changes state is a property of the substance; describe the difference between boiling and evaporation; measure temperature and record results in tables; plan a fair test and work safely; find out that gases take up space and have mass, and use a prediction and its result. Key words: property, melting point, boiling point, evaporation, temperature, gas, mass, fair test.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D lab bench or with sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
