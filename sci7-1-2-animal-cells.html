    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 1 Cells, section 1.2 Animal cells, pages 7–10; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), section 1.2, pages 5–6. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out how animal cells differ from plant cells; use a microscope to look at animal cells (cheek cells), including preparing a slide with methylene blue stain. Key words: stain, cell membrane, cytoplasm, mitochondrion, nucleus, magnify, eyepiece, objective lens, stage, focusing knob, cover slip, cotton bud.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping parts of the animal cell, preparing a cheek-cell slide on the bench, using the microscope, or sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Two ideas to watch: an animal cell's outside layer is only the thin cell membrane (there is no cell wall), and cheek cells are almost clear, which is why the blue stain is needed to see them.
