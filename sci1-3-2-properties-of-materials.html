    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 3 Materials in my world, section 3.2, pages 44–47; Workbook 1, pages 26–28. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out about the properties of materials; observe materials to find out their properties. Key words: property, hard, soft, strong, weak, flexible, rigid, shiny, dull, rough, smooth, threads, sort.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — feeling the samples on the 3D bench, pressing BEND on the two rulers, sorting things into shiny or dull, looking at fabric threads with the magnifying glass and sorting the fabrics from rough to smooth. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the bench' panel only move the camera; doing a step means acting on the bench itself or on the step card. The big idea of this section: the words that describe what a material is like — strong, flexible, shiny, rough — are called its properties.
