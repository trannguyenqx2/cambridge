    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 6 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742979), Unit 3 Rocks, the rock cycle and soil, section 3.2, pages 70–77; Workbook 6 (ISBN 9781108742986), pages 40–42. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: classify rocks into types and describe their features; use diagrams and photographs to describe sedimentary rocks; explain how sedimentary rocks form through weathering, erosion and sedimentation; describe how fossils form in sedimentary rocks; sort and group rocks by observed differences; complete a key to sedimentary rocks. Key words: cast, deposit, sedimentary rock, erode, fossil, sedimentation, mould, preserve, transport, valley, sediment, weathering.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the three weathering agents, following the sediment journey from valley to sea floor in order, using the book's two-question key on a mystery rock, sorting rock descriptions, tapping the three parts of fossil formation, then sorting moulds from casts. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Watch out for the key ideas of this section: sedimentary rocks always form in layers from sediments stuck together, and fossils can only form where a dead organism is buried quickly in sediment in water, with very little air.
