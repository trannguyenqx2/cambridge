    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 5 Forces and magnetism, section 5.3, pages 112–117; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 68–71. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: identify friction, upthrust, air resistance and water resistance (drag); see that an object can have several forces on it even when it is at rest; use force diagrams; predict which parachute will fall faster; repeat measurements, record them in a table and make a conclusion. Key words: friction, air resistance, water resistance, upthrust, drag, streamlined.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — riding the bike, sorting drag forces, tapping the ball and boat in the pond, building the boat force diagram, dropping both parachutes, and choosing the conclusion that fits the recorded results (small parachute trials 1.3, 1.5, 1.4 s, average 1.4 s; large parachute trials 2.7, 2.9, 2.8 s, average 2.8 s). The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
