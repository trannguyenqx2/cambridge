    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 2 Materials and their structure, section 2.4, pages 40–44; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), section 2.4, pages 30–31. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about the water cycle; use scientific words to describe the stages of the water cycle; use particle theory to explain what happens in each part of the cycle. Key words: atmosphere, groundwater, open water, precipitation, surface run-off, transpiration, water cycle, water vapour.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping places in the 3D landscape or sorting cards. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'Look at the landscape' panel only move the camera; to collect evidence, tap the place itself in the scene. The core particle idea runs through every stage: particles that gain energy move faster and can break free (evaporation, melting); particles that lose energy slow down and move closer together (condensation, freezing). The Workbook diagram-labelling exercise is reproduced with its seven stages (a–g); in this panel each label is identified by a written clue, because the printed diagram itself is not part of the digital text.
