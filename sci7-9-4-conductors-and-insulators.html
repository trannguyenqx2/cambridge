    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 9 Electricity, section 9.4, pages 304–310; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 172–173. All wording is rewritten in our own words.

    Objectives: discover the difference between electrical conductors and insulators; learn about uses for conductors and insulators; test some materials for electrical conduction or insulation. Key words: allow current to flow, conduct electricity, conductor, electrons, inhibit, insulator.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action on the 3D circuit bench, with sorting cards, or with check questions. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done. Show me how only demonstrates — it never counts as done.
