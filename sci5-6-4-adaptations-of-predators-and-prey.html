    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 5 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742955), Unit 6 Seasons and adaptations, section 6.4, pages 155–164; Workbook 5 (ISBN 9781108742962), pages 98–101. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book. The section's closing project and the unit check-your-progress pages are classroom research tasks and are not part of this interactive lesson.

    Objectives: describe adaptations of predators and prey; use secondary information sources to research questions; group predators and prey according to their adaptations. Key words: predator, prey, adaptation, camouflage, blend in, pack, teamwork, venom, quills, weapon.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the animals on the 3D savanna itself, or sorting cards into groups. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the savanna' panel only move the camera to look at an animal; meeting an animal means tapping it on the savanna. The examples follow the book's order: the cheetah and the springbok first, then the chameleon, cobra, eagle and wild dogs, the grouping table, the prey defences (hiding, groups, shells, speed), attack with stings, horns and quills, and finally the owl — the Workbook's night hunter.
