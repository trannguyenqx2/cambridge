    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 9 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742863), Unit 4 Maintaining life, section 4.3 Excretion in humans, pages 147–150; Workbook 9 (ISBN 9781108742894), pages 75–76. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives (from the book): find out what substances humans excrete; study the structure of the human excretory system; learn how the kidneys help with excretion. Key words: bladder, excretion, kidneys, renal, urea, ureter, urethra, urine.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — tapping the models on the 3D bench itself, or sorting cards into groups. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the answer stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the bench' panel only move the camera; testing a part means tapping the model on the bench. The steps follow the book's order.
