    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 7 (2nd edition, ISBN 9781108742788), Unit 7 Microorganisms in the environment, section 7.2, pages 232–237; Workbook 7 (ISBN 9781108742818), pages 131–134. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: construct food chains and food webs with arrows showing energy transfer; use the correct terms for the organisms in a chain or web; judge how well chains and webs describe feeding relationships. Key words: carnivore, consumer, ecology, food chain, food web, herbivore, predator, prey, producer.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action — most of them by tapping organisms in the 3D plains scene, in the order energy flows. The lesson checks the action itself (the dark 'Show me' line), and the conclusion stays locked until the action is done. 'Show me how' only demonstrates — it never counts as done. Buttons in the 'On the plains' panel only move the camera; building a chain means tapping the organisms themselves. The key idea to watch: arrows in a food chain point in the direction the energy travels — from the organism that is eaten to the one that eats it.
