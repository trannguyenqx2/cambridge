    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science Learner's Book 8 (2nd edition, 2021; ISBN 9781108742825), Unit 1 Respiration, section 1.5 Blood, pages 25–33; Workbook 8 (ISBN 9781108742856), section 1.5, pages 16–20. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: learn about the structure of blood; find out about the functions of red blood cells, white blood cells and blood plasma. Key words: antibodies, blood plasma, oxyhaemoglobin, pathogens, red blood cells, white blood cells.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action in the 3D scene, with sorting cards or the altitude results table. The lesson checks the action itself, and the answer stays locked until the action is done.
