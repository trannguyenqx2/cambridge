    For teachers and parents — about this lesson

    Source: Based on Cambridge Primary Science Learner's Book 1 (2nd edition), Unit 2 Sound, section 2.3, pages 32–36; Workbook 1, pages 21–22. All wording in this lesson is rewritten in our own words; no text is copied from the book.

    Objectives: find out how sound changes as it moves away from a sound source; compare the sound in different places; show loud and quiet with your hands; stay safe near very loud sounds. Key words: move, near, far away, loud, quiet, safe, comparing.

    How it works: each step asks for one real action, and the lesson checks the action itself (the dark Show-me line); the answer stays locked until the action is done. Show-me-how only demonstrates — it never counts as done. All loudness in this lesson is shown visually (travelling waves and a 5-bar meter), never by real audio, so it works with the sound off. The triangle rings at three places — Near (5 bars), Middle (3 bars) and Far away (1 bar) — and the child records each place in the comparing table. Watch out for the key ideas: a sound gets quieter as it moves away from its source, and a very loud sound can damage ears, so we stand far away or cover our ears.
